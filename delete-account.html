Delete your Adaptive Chess account

Adaptive Chess: Neural Coach (com.ahmadessam.adaptivechess), developed by Ahmad Essam. Last updated: 2 October 2026.

This page explains how to delete your Adaptive Chess account and the data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to ahmadessam1997@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Adaptive Chess account”.
  2. Include the username you play under, so the account can be identified. If you signed in with Google, send the email from that same Google address.
  3. You will receive confirmation once the account has been deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.
To delete everything held on your device instead, open the app and go to Settings → Account → Sign out, then uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data, including your settings, rating and any items you own.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Username Deleted from the game server
Online rating and match results Deleted from the game server, including your leaderboard entry
Account details stored on your device (including any Google name or email used to sign in) Deleted when you sign out or uninstall. This data is held only on your device and is never sent to our server.
Moves played in online games Not retained — they are passed between players in real time and are not stored after a game ends
Server connection logs (including IP address) Kept for up to 30 days after your last connection for security and abuse prevention, then deleted automatically
Advertising and analytics data (from version 1.1.0) Held by Google (AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase) against your device's advertising ID and an app-instance ID — not against your username, name or email address, so it cannot be looked up from an account. Resetting or deleting the advertising ID in Android (Settings → Privacy → Ads) and uninstalling the app separates anything collected later from it. Analytics events are deleted automatically after 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting.
Purchase records Kept by Google Play, not by us. We never receive or store payment details. Purchase history is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by tax and accounting requirements.

Playing without an account

An account is only needed for online ranked play and to start a Neural Coach subscription. You can keep playing Adaptive Chess against the computer after deleting your account; nothing is sent to our game server when you do. The advertising and usage-analytics data described in the Privacy Policy is still collected by Google while you use the app.
