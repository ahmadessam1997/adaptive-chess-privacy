Delete your Adaptive Chess account

Adaptive Chess: Neural Coach (com.ahmadessam.adaptivechess), developed by Ahmad Essam. Effective from 2 October 2026. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

This page explains how to delete your Adaptive Chess account and the data associated with it. It applies to both the Android version (Google Play) and the iPhone version (App Store).

Delete your account in the app

The quickest way, on both Android and iPhone, is in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Since 15 October 2026 this removes the account from your device and asks our game server to delete your username and rating from the public leaderboard, with no email needed. If the server cannot be reached at that moment, the local deletion still completes and the app tells you; in that case send the email request below so we can remove your leaderboard entry.

How to request account deletion by email

  1. Send an email to ahmadessam1997@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Adaptive Chess account”.
  2. Include the username you play under, so the account can be identified. If you signed in with Google, send the email from that same Google address. If you signed in with Apple on iPhone, send it from the email address you used with Apple (if you chose "Hide My Email", please also tell us the username, since we never see your real address).
  3. You will receive confirmation once the account has been deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.
To delete everything held on your device instead, open the app and go to Settings → Account → Sign out (or Delete account), then uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data, including your settings, rating and any items you own.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Username Deleted from the game server
Online rating and match results Deleted from the game server, including your leaderboard entry
Account details stored on your device (including any Google or, on iPhone, Apple name or email used to sign in — or the Apple private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email") Deleted when you delete the account in the app, sign out or uninstall. This data is held only on your device and is never sent to our server.
Moves played in online games Not retained — they are passed between players in real time and are not stored after a game ends
Server connection logs (including IP address) Kept for up to 30 days after your last connection for security and abuse prevention, then deleted automatically
Advertising and analytics data (from version 1.1.0) Held by Google (AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase) against your device's advertising ID and an app-instance ID — not against your username, name or email address, so it cannot be looked up from an account. Resetting or deleting the advertising ID in Android (Settings → Privacy → Ads), or on iPhone turning off tracking for the app (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the iPhone version reads the advertising identifier only if you allowed tracking), and uninstalling the app separates anything collected later from it. Analytics events are deleted automatically after 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting.
Purchase records Kept by Google Play (Android) or by Apple / the App Store (iPhone), not by us. We never receive or store payment details. Purchase history is governed by Google's Privacy Policy or Apple's Privacy Policy and by tax and accounting requirements. On iPhone the Neural Coach subscription is managed or cancelled in iOS Settings → Subscriptions; deleting your account does not cancel it.

Playing without an account

An account is only needed for online ranked play and to start a Neural Coach subscription. You can keep playing Adaptive Chess against the computer after deleting your account; nothing is sent to our game server when you do. The advertising and usage-analytics data described in the Privacy Policy is still collected by Google while you use the app.
