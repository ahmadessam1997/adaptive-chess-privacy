Privacy Policy — Adaptive Chess

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Adaptive Chess ("the app", package com.ahmadessam.adaptivechess) is developed by Ahmad Essam. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Adaptive Chess does not collect personal information, does not require an account, and does not send any data off your device. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK in the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves gameplay data locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This includes your player rating, game settings, selected board/skin, and which optional items you own. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (the Neural Coach subscription and cosmetic skins) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; those are handled entirely by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is rated for everyone and does not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect data (for example, online multiplayer), this policy and the app's Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.
