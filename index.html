Privacy Policy — Adaptive Chess

Effective from 3 October 2026 (applies from version 1.2.0). Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Adaptive Chess ("the app", package com.ahmadessam.adaptivechess) is developed by Ahmad Essam. This policy explains what data the app handles.

This policy covers both the Android version (Google Play) and the iPhone version (App Store) of the app. They are the same game; where the two platforms differ, the text says so.

You can play without an account. Since version 1.1.0 the app shows advertising and measures how it is used, which means some data is sent to Google even if you never sign in. The sections below list exactly what.

Data collected for everyone who uses the app

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional rewarded ads that you start yourself (to play one Coach Mode game, review a game or try a skin for one game). To deliver and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android: the Google advertising ID; iPhone: the IDFA, and only if you allow tracking — see below);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country / region / city) is derived;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version) and diagnostic data about ad delivery;
  • which ads you were shown and whether you interacted with them.

Google may use this to show personalised ads, where the law and your choices allow it. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app (the entry is shown where a choice applies).

On iPhone (App Tracking Transparency). After the consent form (where it applies), the iPhone version shows Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt once. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised and the app does not read your advertising identifier (IDFA). Google's SKAdNetwork is used to attribute ad installs in a way that does not identify you. You can change your choice later in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The iPhone version does not use the Android advertising-ID permission described in the Android-specific paragraph below.

Your controls. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the Android app requests the AD_ID permission to read it; this is Android-specific). On iPhone use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking as described above. Buying Remove Ads or holding an active Neural Coach subscription turns off the banner and the between-game ads; the advertising SDK stays in the app so that the optional rewarded ads keep working, and it may still contact Google to prepare them.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is used and to improve it, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase. The events are: first open and session information; menu choices; game start and end (mode, level, result, number of moves, duration); daily-puzzle start and result (including your streak length); opening the store or the subscription screen; purchase started / completed (product name only); ads shown and rewarded ads completed; the rating prompt; online matchmaking; friend games (same-phone or online-with-a-code: started, created/joined, ended, result and number of moves); and sign-in completed (method only). Along with them the Firebase SDK collects an app-instance ID, the advertising ID, device and operating-system information, and your approximate location (country level, from the IP address). These events do not contain your name, email address, username or the moves you play. The iPhone version sends the same events; on iPhone the Firebase SDK does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Data collected only if you sign in and play online

An account is optional. It is asked for only when you open Online Play or start a Neural Coach subscription. You can create an account with a username and password, or sign in with Google; the iPhone version also offers Sign in with Apple.

Your email address (including any Apple private relay address) and name are not sent to our game server and are not shared with other players. Whichever sign-in method you use, the only account data that leaves your device is your chosen username and your rating, for online play. Other players only ever see your username, rating and chosen piece skin. A practice match against the bot, offered when no online opponent is found, is played entirely on your device: nothing about it is sent to the game server and it does not change your online rating.

Playing a friend

Same phone. A pass-and-play game on one device is played entirely on that device: nothing about it is sent to our game server.

Online with a code. You can play a friend online without an account. When you create or join a game with a code, the app sends to our game server the name shown to your friend — your username if you are signed in, otherwise a random guest name such as "Guest 4821" that the app generates and keeps on your device — together with your in-app rating, your chosen piece skin and the moves you play. Your friend sees that name, rating and skin. Friend games are unranked: they do not change any rating, are not added to the leaderboard, and the moves are relayed in real time and not kept after the game. An unused game code expires after 15 minutes. As with any network connection, your IP address is processed by our server and hosting provider.

Data stored on your device

The app saves gameplay data locally using standard on-device storage: your player rating, game settings, selected board/skin, which optional items you own, your daily-puzzle results and streak, counters used to decide when an ad or the rating prompt may appear, your reminder preference, your guest name for friend games and your same-phone board preference, and (if you signed in) your account details (including the name and email address received from Google or, on iPhone, from Apple). If you create an account with a username and password instead of Google or Apple, the password is kept only on your device, in scrambled (hashed) form, and is never sent to us. On iPhone the rating prompt is Apple's own review request sheet, which collects nothing for us; the app only keeps the local counter that decides when it may be shown. All of this is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

After you solve your first daily puzzle the app offers a daily reminder. If you accept, Android or iOS asks for permission to show notifications (the permission is requested only when the reminder is first used). The reminders are scheduled on your device; the app does not use a push-notification service and no notification token or identifier is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in the app's Settings, in Android's notification settings, or on iPhone under iOS Settings → Notifications.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Data is shared with, or processed by, only the services needed to run and fund the app:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (advertising ID, IP address / approximate location, device information, ad interactions). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics (app-instance ID, advertising ID, usage events, device information).
  • Google Play (Android) — processes purchases and, if you use it, Google Sign-In. On iPhone, Google Sign-In (if you use it) is still provided by Google.
  • Apple (iPhone) — the App Store processes purchases (Apple In-App Purchase) and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Fly.io — hosts our online game server. See Fly.io's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Your username and rating are kept on the game server while your account is active, so your ranking persists between sessions. Game moves are relayed in real time and are not stored after a game ends. Server connection logs are retained for a short period for abuse prevention. Advertising and analytics data is held by Google under its own retention rules; analytics events are kept for 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting and are then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account and data

You can sign out and delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account in the app, on both Android and iPhone. Since 15 October 2026, Delete account removes the account from your device and asks our game server to delete your username and rating from the public leaderboard. If the server cannot be reached at that moment, the local deletion still completes and the app tells you; in that case use our account deletion page or email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete your username and rating within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android's settings, or turning off tracking for the app in iOS settings, separates future advertising and analytics data from what was collected before. Analytics events are not linked to your name, email address or username; they expire automatically as described above, and you can email us at the same address to ask for earlier deletion of whatever can be matched to your installation.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (the Neural Coach subscription, Remove Ads and cosmetic skins) are processed by Google Play on Android and by the App Store (Apple In-App Purchase) on iPhone. We do not receive or store your payment details; those are handled entirely by Google or Apple. On iPhone, purchases are tied to your Apple Account, the Neural Coach subscription is an App Store auto-renewable subscription that you manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Subscriptions, and Restore purchases in the app's store restores items bought with the same Apple Account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Online play and sign-in are optional; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is collected, this policy, the app's Play Store data-safety section and its App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
