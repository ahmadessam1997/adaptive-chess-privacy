Privacy Policy — Adaptive Chess

Last updated: 2 October 2026 (applies from version 1.1.0)

Adaptive Chess ("the app", package com.ahmadessam.adaptivechess) is developed by Ahmad Essam. This policy explains what data the app handles.

You can play without an account. Since version 1.1.0 the app shows advertising and measures how it is used, which means some data is sent to Google even if you never sign in. The sections below list exactly what.

Data collected for everyone who uses the app

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional rewarded ads that you start yourself (to play one Coach Mode game, review a game or try a skin for one game). To deliver and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country / region / city) is derived;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version) and diagnostic data about ad delivery;
  • which ads you were shown and whether you interacted with them.

Google may use this to show personalised ads, where the law and your choices allow it. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app (the entry is shown where a choice applies).

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Buying Remove Ads or holding an active Neural Coach subscription turns off the banner and the between-game ads; the advertising SDK stays in the app so that the optional rewarded ads keep working, and it may still contact Google to prepare them.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is used and to improve it, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase. The events are: first open and session information; menu choices; game start and end (mode, level, result, number of moves, duration); daily-puzzle start and result (including your streak length); opening the store or the subscription screen; purchase started / completed (product name only); ads shown and rewarded ads completed; the rating prompt; online matchmaking; and sign-in completed (method only). Along with them the Firebase SDK collects an app-instance ID, the advertising ID, device and operating-system information, and your approximate location (country level, from the IP address). These events do not contain your name, email address, username or the moves you play. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Data collected only if you sign in and play online

An account is optional. It is asked for only when you open Online Play or start a Neural Coach subscription.

Your email address is not sent to our game server and is not shared with other players. Other players only ever see your username, rating and chosen piece skin. A practice match against the bot, offered when no online opponent is found, is played entirely on your device: nothing about it is sent to the game server and it does not change your online rating.

Data stored on your device

The app saves gameplay data locally using standard on-device storage: your player rating, game settings, selected board/skin, which optional items you own, your daily-puzzle results and streak, counters used to decide when an ad or the rating prompt may appear, your reminder preference, and (if you signed in) your account details. If you create an account with a username and password instead of Google, the password is kept only on your device, in scrambled (hashed) form, and is never sent to us. All of this is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

After you solve your first daily puzzle the app offers a daily reminder. If you accept, Android asks for permission to show notifications. The reminders are scheduled on your device; the app does not use a push-notification service and no notification token or identifier is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Data is shared with, or processed by, only the services needed to run and fund the app:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (advertising ID, IP address / approximate location, device information, ad interactions). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics (app-instance ID, advertising ID, usage events, device information).
  • Google Play — processes purchases and, if you use it, Google Sign-In.
  • Fly.io — hosts our online game server. See Fly.io's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Your username and rating are kept on the game server while your account is active, so your ranking persists between sessions. Game moves are relayed in real time and are not stored after a game ends. Server connection logs are retained for a short period for abuse prevention. Advertising and analytics data is held by Google under its own retention rules; analytics events are kept for 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting and are then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account and data

You can sign out and delete your local account at any time from Settings → Account in the app. To have your username and rating permanently erased from our game server, email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete them within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android's settings separates future advertising and analytics data from what was collected before. Analytics events are not linked to your name, email address or username; they expire automatically as described above, and you can email us at the same address to ask for earlier deletion of whatever can be matched to your installation.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (the Neural Coach subscription, Remove Ads and cosmetic skins) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; those are handled entirely by Google.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Online play and sign-in are optional; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is collected, this policy and the app's Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.
